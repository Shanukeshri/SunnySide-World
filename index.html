Controls & Survival Guide

  • WASD or Arrows — Move character
  • Shift (Hold) — Sprint (Consumes Stamina)
  • Left Click — Attack with weapon / Chop tree / Mine rock
  • 1 to 8 — Select Hotbar tool/item
  • E — Interact (Pet wildlife, Talk to villagers, Open chest/door)
  • F — Feed nearby wildlife (Wheat/Berries) or Eat selected food
  • I or C — Open Backpack & Crafting Workshop
  • B — Toggle Building Placement Mode (Walls, Campfire, Chest)
  • Space — Jump / Hop
  • ESC — Pause / Help menu
