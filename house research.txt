--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 THE CURRENT SYSTEM VS. DESIRED ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the current RTSGame engine:
- Buildings are spawned as monolithic rectangular bounding boxes (e.g. 3x3, 4x3,
  or 4x4 tiles) defined in `SettlementGenerator.ts` (`house_cottage_01`,
  `house_farmhouse_01`, `house_barn_01`, etc.).
- They are drawn by `SettlementRenderer.ts` using pre-baked static exterior roof
  sprites sliced from `houses.png`.
- The entire bounding box is marked `blocked = true` in the world grid.
- Consequently, buildings are opaque, impenetrable obstacles. Characters cannot
  enter them, villagers cannot live or sleep inside them, and there is no interior
  space, furniture, or architectural detail.

The desired architecture replaces these static exterior blocks with open-interior,
cutaway 2.5D detailed buildings inspired by games like Necesse, RimWorld, and
Stardew Valley:
- Cutaway Walls: Top and side walls provide physical presence and elevation framing
  without obscuring the player's view of interior rooms.
- Walkable Interiors: Buildings have open doorways, threshold mats, and walkable
  floors so players and villagers can enter seamlessly without loading screens.
- Functional Furniture: Real beds where villagers sleep at night, dining tables
  with oriented chairs, cooking stoves, storage chests, bookcases, and baths.
- Architectural Variety: Buildings feature multiple rooms (e.g. living quarters,
  stone cellars with ladders leading underground) and attached exterior annexes
  (fenced crop gardens, walled animal pens, outdoor campfire patios).
- Procedural Flexibility: Given ANY input dimensions (W x H) requested by the
  settlement planner, the algorithm produces a complete, aesthetically cohesive,
  and 100% pathable building every time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 CORE ARCHITECTURAL REQUIREMENTS & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To reproduce this depth procedurally without manual hand-crafting, the algorithm
must satisfy five non-negotiable principles:
1. Strict 2.5D Cutaway Perspective:
   North (back) walls have 2-tile height (roof cap + facade face) to hold windows,
   mirrors, paintings, and headboards. South (front) walls are low-profile so they
   never occlude characters or furniture inside.
2. Semantic Room Hierarchy:
   Space is partitioned logically: Main Living/Hearth -> Private Bedroom -> Stone
   Cellar / Workshop -> Attached Yard (Garden or Pen).
3. Constraint-Based Furniture Placement:
   Every furniture piece has strict physical requirements: beds must touch walls;
   stoves must align with rooflines for chimney smoke; chairs must face tables with
   approach clearance; rugs must not block doors.
4. Guaranteed Walkability & Reachability:
   BFS flood-fill verification must prove that every room, bed, chair, chest, stove,
   and ladder can be reached from the front door with at least 1 tile of clear floor.
5. Deterministic Randomness:
   Given the same `(W, H, seed)`, the generator must produce the exact same layout,
   enabling reliable multiplayer replication and compact save states.

3. THE PROCEDURAL HOUSE GENERATION ALGORITHM
===============================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 INPUT PARAMETERS & DIMENSION NORMALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The generation function is defined as a pure, deterministic pipeline:
```typescript
function generateProceduralDetailedHouse(
  targetW: number,
  targetH: number,
  seed: number,
  options?: {
    theme?: 'stone' | 'timber' | 'hybrid';
    annexPreference?: 'none' | 'crop_garden' | 'animal_pen' | 'cellar_only';
    forceLShaped?: boolean;
  }
): DetailedHouseData;
```

Parameter Constraints:
- Minimum Dimensions: `targetW >= 6`, `targetH >= 6` (interior 4x4 minimum).
- Standard Single-Room Cottage: `7x7` to `8x8`.
- Medium Two-Room Homestead: `9x7` to `11x9`.
- Large Estate / Manor with Annex: `12x10` to `16x14`.
- PRNG: Mulberry32 seeded from `seed`. All layout choices, room splits, themes,
  and prop selections are 100% reproducible.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 STEP 1: FOOTPRINT DECOMPOSITION & COMPLEX MORPHOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instead of forcing every building into a rigid rectangle, the algorithm chooses
a morphological footprint based on the available size:

A. Small Footprints (W <= 8 and H <= 8):
   - Morphology: `RECTANGLE_COMPACT`.
   - The entire bounding box is the interior house structure.
   - Annex: Micro-exterior trim only (outdoor campfire, bench beside door).

B. Medium Footprints (W = 9..11, H = 7..9):
   - 60% probability: `RECTANGLE_EXPANDED` (Split into Main Hall + Bedroom or Cellar).
   - 40% probability: `L_SHAPED_WITH_GARDEN`.
     * Main Hall: Dimensions `(W - 3) x H`.
     * Attached Wing / Cellar: Dimensions `3 x (H - 3)`.
     * The remaining `3 x 3` corner becomes an attached fenced crop garden or patio!

C. Large Footprints (W >= 12, H >= 10):
   - 45% probability: `L_SHAPED_ESTATE` with attached fenced crop garden or animal pen.
   - 35% probability: `COMPOUND_MANOR` (Main living block + attached stone cellar wing
     + attached walled courtyard).
   - 20% probability: `GREAT_HALL_RECTANGLE` (3-4 grand interior rooms).

D. Theme Assignment:
   - If not specified in `options`, select theme deterministically:
     * `seed % 2 === 0` -> `THEME_TIMBER` (Log walls, wood plank floor, rustic farm feel).
     * `seed % 2 === 1` -> `THEME_STONE` (Stone brick walls, wood/stone floor, sturdy manor).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 STEP 2: SEMANTIC SPACE PARTITIONING (BSP & ROOM ASSIGNMENT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the interior space of the house hull (excluding exterior perimeter walls), let:
`innerW = houseHullW - 2`, `innerH = houseHullH - 2`.

Partitioning Rules:
1. Single Room Threshold:
   - If `innerW < 6` or `innerH < 5`:
     * Assign entire space to `ROOM_STUDIO` (open-plan living, dining, and sleeping).
2. Two-Room Split:
   - If `innerW >= 6` and `innerH >= 5`:
     * Evaluate split axis:
       - If `innerW >= innerH`: Vertical split along column `splitX = floor(innerW * 0.58)`.
       - If `innerH > innerW`: Horizontal split along row `splitY = floor(innerH * 0.55)`.
     * Room Roles:
       - Room 1 (Larger, contains front door): `ROOM_GREAT_HALL` (Living/Kitchen/Dining).
       - Room 2 (Smaller, private):
         * 50% probability: `ROOM_BEDROOM` (Master sleeping quarters).
         * 50% probability: `ROOM_CELLAR` (Stone floor dungeon ladder & storage).
3. Three-Room Split:
   - If `innerW >= 10` and `innerH >= 7`:
     * Primary split divides living space from private quarters.
     * Secondary split in private quarters creates:
       - `ROOM_BEDROOM` (Sleeping, wardrobe, desk).
       - `ROOM_CELLAR` (Stone floor, ladder to dungeon, barrels).
4. Attached Outdoor Annex Assignment:
   - If the footprint has an open courtyard or attached yard:
     * If Theme == Stone: Assign to `YARD_ANIMAL_PEN` (enclosed stone wall, grass, cow/sheep).
     * If Theme == Timber: Assign to `YARD_CROP_GARDEN` (wooden fence, tilled soil, crops).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 STEP 3: PERIMETER & PARTITION WALL AUTOTILING (2.5D ELEVATION MODEL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A top-down 2.5D cutaway building must balance architectural mass with visibility.
The algorithm implements the following elevation rules:

1. North Wall (Back Wall) - 2 Tiles Tall:
   - Row 0 (Exterior Roof Cap):
     * Left corner: `lip_nw`
     * Mid spans: `lip_n`
     * Right corner: `lip_ne`
     * Collision: `isBlocked = true`.
     * Visual Role: Represents the top edge of the cutaway wall and roof overhang.
   - Row 1 (Vertical Wall Facade):
     * Left edge: `facade_w`
     * Mid spans: `facade_solid` (or `facade_window` if aligned with open floor).
     * Right edge: `facade_e`
     * Collision: `isBlocked = true` (characters cannot walk through the wall base).
     * Mounting Layer: This visible face is where headboards, windows, brass wall sconces,
       paintings, and vanity mirrors are anchored.

2. West & East Walls (Side Flanks) - 1 Tile Wide:
   - Columns `x = 0` and `x = houseHullW - 1` from row 2 down to row `houseHullH - 2`.
   - Tile: `wall_west` and `wall_east`.
   - Collision: `isBlocked = true`.
   - Casts a subtle 2-pixel inner shadow onto adjacent interior floor tiles.

3. South Wall (Front Facade) - 1 Tile Tall:
   - Row `houseHullH - 1` across all columns.
   - Left corner: `corner_sw`
   - Mid spans: `wall_south`
   - Right corner: `corner_se`
   - Collision: `isBlocked = true` except at doorway cutouts.
   - Visual Role: Low-profile framing that does not obscure characters or furniture.

4. Interior Partition Walls - 1 Tile Wide:
   - Placed along `splitX` or `splitY` between rooms.
   - Built using thin `Inner Walls` partition tiles (`partition_col`, `partition_row`).
   - Automatically autotiles T-junction joins where partitions meet North or South walls.
   - Carves a 1-tile wide doorway cutout with post end caps.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 STEP 4: CIRCULATION SPINE, EXTERIOR & INTERIOR DOORWAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Circulation guarantees that the house is a pleasure to navigate:

1. Exterior Front Doorway:
   - Location: Placed on the South Wall (row `houseHullH - 1`).
   - Column: `doorX = clamp(floor(houseHullW * 0.45), 2, houseHullW - 3)`.
   - Room Alignment: Must open into `ROOM_GREAT_HALL` (never directly into a private
     bedroom or cellar).
   - Tile: Wooden Door Threshold Mat (`door_mat`, [x:272, y:272]).
   - Collision: `isBlocked = false` (100% walkable).
   - Exterior Apron: Place a 1-tile stone/cobble step directly outside `(doorX, houseHullH)`.

2. Interior Partition Doorways:
   - In every partition wall, carve an opening at least 1 tile wide.
   - Position: Placed along the natural line of sight from the front door.
   - Tile: Replaced with the adjoining room's floor tile or a wood threshold slat.
   - Collision: `isBlocked = false`.

3. Yard / Courtyard Access:
   - If an attached crop garden or animal pen is present:
     * Place a fence gate (`fence_gate`, 1 tile wide, walkable) on the perimeter fence.
     * Optionally place a side/back door in the house wall leading directly into the yard.

4. Guaranteed Circulation Spine:
   - A 1-tile wide corridor extending from the front door, through all interior doors,
     to the cellar ladder and yard gate is flagged as `CLEARANCE_CORRIDOR`.
   - No solid furniture may EVER be placed in this corridor!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 STEP 5: FLOOR TEXTURING & AREA RUG PLACEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Floors give each room its distinct identity:
1. Floor Assignment by Room Type:
   - `ROOM_GREAT_HALL` / `ROOM_LIVING`: Warm Timber Planks (`floor_wood`) or Stone Flagstone.
   - `ROOM_BEDROOM` / `ROOM_STUDY`: Wood Parquet (`floor_parquet`) or Timber Planks.
   - `ROOM_CELLAR`: Cool Grey Flagstone / Dungeon Cobblestone (`floor_stone`).
   - `YARD_CROP_GARDEN`: Dark Tilled Soil (`tilled_soil.png`).
   - `YARD_ANIMAL_PEN`: Natural Meadow Grass (`grass_textured_01`).
2. Area Rug Placement:
   - Rugs are non-blocking floor overlays (`isBlocked = false`).
   - Algorithm finds the largest rectangle of open floor in a room:
     * In Living Room: Center a Blue Geometric Runner Rug (1x3) or Blue Rectangular
       Carpet (2x3).
     * In Bedroom: Center a Pink Heart Rug (3x2) or Woven Fiber Mat (2x2).
     * In front of Fireplace: Center a Dark Brown Fur Rug (3x2).
   - Constraint: Rug must be at least 1 tile away from the front door to prevent visual crowding.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.7 STEP 6: SEMANTIC ANCHOR IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before spawning furniture, the algorithm analyzes every cell `(x, y)` in the room
and assigns architectural anchor tags:
- `ANCHOR_NORTH_WALL`: Cells along row 1 (against the North wall facade).
  * Ideal for: Bed headboards, bathtubs, cooking stoves, fireplaces, wardrobes.
- `ANCHOR_WEST_WALL` / `ANCHOR_EAST_WALL`: Cells against side perimeter or partition walls.
  * Ideal for: Bookcases, study desks, dressers, storage chests, candelabras.
- `ANCHOR_CORNER`: Corners where two walls meet.
  * Ideal for: Potted plants, storage barrels, standing candelabras, wash basins.
- `ANCHOR_CENTER_FLOOR`: Cells with at least 1 tile of clearance in all 4 cardinal directions.
  * Ideal for: Dining tables with chairs, cellar ladders, central area rugs.
- `CLEARANCE_CORRIDOR`: Cells along the primary walking spines between doors.
  * Strictly reserved for open walking floor or rugs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.8 STEP 7: CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION FURNITURE SLOTTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Furniture is placed room by room using a prioritized constraint satisfaction solver.

A. In `ROOM_BEDROOM` (or Studio Quarters):
   1. Primary Bed:
      - Select Master Double Bed (2x2) or Single Bed (1x2).
      - Slot against `ANCHOR_NORTH_WALL` (headboard at row 1).
      - Ensure foot of bed and at least one lateral side are free walkable floor.
   2. Bedside Nightstand & Lighting:
      - In the 1-tile gap between the bed and the side wall, spawn a Nightstand (1x1).
      - Place a lit Table Candle or 3-Candle Candelabra on or beside the nightstand.
   3. Wardrobe / Armoire (2x2):
      - Slot flush against an unblocked section of North or side wall.
   4. Study Desk (2x1) & Chair:
      - Slot against side wall with red cushion chair facing the desk.
   5. Wall Decor:
      - Vanity Mirror or Framed Painting mounted at row 1 above the dresser or desk.

B. In `ROOM_GREAT_HALL` (Living & Dining):
   1. Hearth Station (Cook Stove or Fireplace):
      - Slot against `ANCHOR_NORTH_WALL`.
      - Width: 1x2 (Stove) or 2x2 (Fireplace).
      - Alignment: Must record roofline coordinates for chimney smoke VFX.
      - Front clearance: 1 tile in front must be clear floor for cooking interaction.
   2. Dining Table Ensemble:
      - Horizontal Table (2x1) or Square Table (2x2) placed at `ANCHOR_CENTER_FLOOR`.
      - Place 1-2 Chairs North of table facing South (`chair_s`).
      - Place 1-2 Chairs South of table facing North (`chair_n`).
      - Ensure the tile behind each chair is walkable so characters can approach and sit.
   3. Storage Chest & Bookcase:
      - Place Wooden Storage Chest (1x1) along wall or in corner.
      - Place Bookcase (1x2) flush against partition wall.
   4. Wall Lighting:
      - Scan North wall: every 2-3 tiles of open wall receives a Brass Lit Wall Sconce.

C. In `ROOM_CELLAR`:
   1. Subterranean Ladder / Trapdoor:
      - Placed at center of the stone room.
      - Footprint: 1x1 tile.
      - All 4 surrounding neighbor tiles must be clear stone floor for easy approach.
   2. Cellar Storage:
      - Place Closed Barrel (`furn_barrel_closed`) in one corner.
      - Place Open Barrel (`furn_barrel_open`) or Weapons Rack in another corner.
      - Place Blacksmith Anvil (2x1) if cellar serves as a forge workshop.
   3. Lighting:
      - Mount a Wall Torch on the stone wall.

D. In `YARD_CROP_GARDEN`:
   1. Soil Tilling:
      - Fill interior yard cells with tilled soil tiles (`tilled_soil.png`).
   2. Plant Crops:
      - Spawn crop stalks (corn, wheat, carrots) with growth stage 2 or 3.
   3. Fence Trim:
      - Enclose perimeter with wooden fences (`fence_wood_h`, `fence_wood_v`).
      - Place swinging wooden gate on the most accessible side.
      - Plant a row of tall sunflowers outside along the front fence.

E. In `YARD_ANIMAL_PEN`:
   1. Enclosure:
      - Enclose with stone walls or wooden rails.
      - Place an iron/wood gate.
   2. Livestock:
      - Spawn 1-2 friendly farm animals (cow, sheep) with gentle wandering AI.
   3. Props:
      - Wooden water trough (2x1) and hay pile.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.9 STEP 8: PATHFINDING & REACHABILITY VERIFICATION (FLOOD FILL SOLVER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No house is accepted unless it passes complete reachability verification:

```
Algorithm ReachabilitySolver(house):
  1. Initialize queue Q = [ house.exteriorDoorPosition ]
  2. Visited = Set( house.exteriorDoorPosition )
  3. While Q is not empty:
       current = Q.popFront()
       For each neighbor in cardinalNeighbors(current):
         If neighbor is inside house bounds
            AND neighbor is not Visited
            AND neighbor.isBlocked == false:
              Visited.add(neighbor)
              Q.push(neighbor)

  4. Validation Assertions:
     - Assertion 1: All rooms have Visited.count(room) >= 0.75 * totalFloor(room)
     - Assertion 2: Bed has at least 1 Visited neighbor tile.
     - Assertion 3: Dining Table has at least 2 Visited chair positions.
     - Assertion 4: Stove / Fireplace has Visited front tile.
     - Assertion 5: Cellar Ladder has at least 3 Visited neighbor tiles.
     - Assertion 6: Yard Gate has Visited path from house exterior.

  5. Auto-Correction on Failure:
     - If any assertion fails:
       * Identify the obstacle cell causing the blockage.
       * Shift furniture 1 tile toward adjacent wall.
       * If still blocked, shrink 2x2 table to 2x1 table.
       * If still blocked, remove non-essential prop (e.g. potted plant or side chair).
       * Repeat ReachabilitySolver until 100% valid.
```

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.10 STEP 9: COLLISION BOUNDARIES, INTERACTION TRIGGERS, LIGHTS & VFX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The generator outputs rich metadata for the RTSGame engine:

1. Collision Registration:
   - Perimeter Walls: `isBlocked = true` (blocks player, NPC, projectiles).
   - Exterior Door: `isBlocked = false` (allows free passage).
   - Full Obstacles: Wardrobes, stoves, fireplaces, bookcases, barrels -> `isBlocked = true`.
   - Sub-Tile Obstacles:
     * Beds: Headboard (top tile) is solid; foot (bottom tile) allows sub-tile entry.
     * Tables: Solid center; chairs are walkable when approaching.
   - Walkable Props: Rugs, doormats, wall sconces -> `isBlocked = false`.

2. Interaction Handlers:
   - Bed: Press [E] -> Initiates player sleep (fades screen, advances time to 06:00).
   - Stove / Hearth: Press [E] -> Opens village cooking / baking crafting menu.
   - Storage Chest / Wardrobe: Press [E] -> Opens 16-slot inventory storage.
   - Cellar Ladder: Press [E] -> Teleports player to underground mine/cellar dungeon.
   - Bathtub: Press [E] -> Restores stamina / grants "Refreshed" buff.

3. Dynamic Night Lighting Nodes:
   - Brass Wall Sconce: Position `(x, y)`, Radius: 4.5 tiles, Color: `#ffaa44`, Intensity: 0.85.
   - 3-Candle Candelabra: Position `(x, y)`, Radius: 5.0 tiles, Color: `#ffcc66`, Intensity: 0.90.
   - Fireplace / Stove: Position `(x, y)`, Radius: 6.0 tiles, Color: `#ff7722`, Intensity: 1.0 (with gentle flicker).
   - Cellar Wall Torch: Position `(x, y)`, Radius: 4.0 tiles, Color: `#ff9933`, Intensity: 0.80.

4. Atmospheric VFX Emitters:
   - Chimney Smoke Emitter:
     * Position: Directly above the fireplace/stove on the roof layer.
     * Emits soft grey smoke particles floating upward with wind drift.
   - Night Candle Flicker:
     * Gentle procedural sine wave luminance oscillation for lit candles.

5. COMPREHENSIVE IMPLEMENTATION PLAN FOR RTSGAME (NO CODE CHANGES YET)
================================================================================
The following architecture details how this procedural generation engine will be
constructed and integrated into RTSGame without touching existing production code
until the research plan is approved.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 MODULE STRUCTURE & INTERFACE CONTRACTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The new system will be encapsulated in dedicated modules under `src/generation/`:

1. `src/generation/HouseInteriorGenerator.ts`:
   - Pure functional layout engine.
   - Core interfaces:
     ```typescript
     export interface HouseTile {
       relX: number;
       relY: number;
       layer: 'ground' | 'floor' | 'wall' | 'partition' | 'door' | 'fence';
       crop: { x: number; y: number; w: number; h: number };
       isBlocked: boolean;
       isDoor?: boolean;
       isGate?: boolean;
     }

     export interface PlacedFurniture {
       id: string;
       type: string;
       name: string;
       relX: number;
       relY: number;
       w: number;
       h: number;
       crop: { x: number; y: number; w: number; h: number };
       isBlocked: boolean;
       interactive?: boolean;
       action?: 'sleep' | 'cook' | 'storage' | 'cellar_descend' | 'wash' | 'sit';
       light?: { radius: number; color: string; intensity: number };
     }

     export interface YardAnnexData {
       type: 'crop_garden' | 'animal_pen' | 'patio';
       bounds: { relX: number; relY: number; w: number; h: number };
       gate: { relX: number; relY: number };
       crops?: Array<{ relX: number; relY: number; cropId: string; stage: number }>;
       animals?: Array<{ species: 'cow' | 'sheep'; relX: number; relY: number }>;
     }

     export interface DetailedHouseData {
       w: number;
       h: number;
       theme: 'stone' | 'timber';
       door: { x: number; y: number };
       tiles: HouseTile[];
       furniture: PlacedFurniture[];
       yard?: YardAnnexData;
       lights: Array<{ relX: number; relY: number; radius: number; color: string; intensity: number }>;
       chimney?: { relX: number; relY: number };
       cellarEntrance?: { relX: number; relY: number };
     }
     ```

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 INTEGRATION WITH SETTLEMENTGENERATOR.TS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In `SettlementGenerator.ts`:
1. Footprint Allocation:
   - When the settlement generator places houses along roads (in `generateHouses()`),
     replace the hardcoded 3x3 / 4x3 static sizes with procedural requests:
     * Small cottage: `W = 7, H = 7`
     * Family homestead: `W = 10, H = 8`
     * Manor / Estate with yard: `W = 14, H = 10`
2. Generator Execution:
   - Call `const detailedHouse = generateProceduralDetailedHouse(W, H, houseSeed);`
3. World Grid Stamping:
   - The tiles (floors, walls, doors, fences) and furniture are stamped into the
     world grid.
   - The house front door position is recorded in the settlement metadata, and the
     existing road path joiner connects the front door directly to the village road!
   - Mark `blocked = true` ONLY on actual wall and solid furniture cells; interior
     floor cells remain `blocked = false`!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 RENDERING PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE IN SETTLEMENTRENDERER.TS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In `SettlementRenderer.ts`, replace the opaque `ctx.drawImage(housesImg, ...)` call
with a multi-layer cutaway rendering pipeline:

- Layer 1 (Base Flooring & Outdoor Ground):
  * Renders interior hardwood planks, wood parquet, flagstone, tilled soil, and yard grass.
  * Renders area rugs (runner rugs, heart rugs, fur rugs) directly on the floor.
  * Renders door threshold mats.

- Layer 2 (Wall Structure & Perimeter Framing):
  * Renders North roof top cap (Row 0) and vertical facade face (Row 1) with windows.
  * Renders West and East side walls with subtle 2px inner floor shadow.
  * Renders South low-profile front wall with doorway cutout.
  * Renders interior partition walls with T-junction joins.
  * Renders yard fences and swinging gates.

- Layer 3 (Y-Depth Sorted Entity Pass):
  * Dynamic Y-depth sorted list combining:
    1. Furniture (beds, tables, chairs, dressers, stoves, cellar ladder, barrels).
    2. Characters (Player, Villager NPCs, Pets).
    3. Livestock animals inside attached pens.
  * Sorting formula: `ySort = entity.y + entity.height`.
  * Guarantees that characters walk BEHIND tables and beds correctly and stand IN FRONT
    of North wall headboards naturally!

- Layer 4 (Atmosphere, Chimney Smoke & Night Lighting):
  * Renders chimney smoke particles drifting from roof coordinates above the stove.
  * In night render pass, creates warm circular light cutouts for brass wall sconces,
    candelabras, fireplaces, and street lanterns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 COLLISION & MOVEMENT ENGINE INTEGRATION IN WORLDMANAGER.TS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In `WorldManager.ts` / `canMoveTo()`:
1. Walkable Thresholds:
   - Characters can step directly through the front doorway into the house.
2. Obstacle Mask:
   - Walls, tall wardrobes, stoves, and bookshelves block movement.
   - Bed footboards, chairs, and rugs allow smooth sub-tile movement.
3. Subterranean Transition:
   - Stepping on or pressing [E] near the Cellar Ladder triggers a transition event
     to the subterranean cave/mine map.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.5 LIFEFORM NPC SCHEDULES & PLAYER INTERACTION ROUTINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Villager Housing Ownership:
   - During settlement generation, each villager NPC is assigned an "owned" house
     and a specific interior bed.
2. Villager Daily Routine:
   - 06:00: Villager wakes up, gets out of bed, walks out the front door to work
     (farming in the attached garden or tending animals in the pen).
   - 12:00: Villager returns to the house, sits in a dining chair to eat lunch.
   - 18:00: Villager sits by the hearth or studies at the desk.
   - 21:00: Villager enters bedroom, lies down in the bed, and spawns sleeping "Zzz" VFX.
